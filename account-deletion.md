# Account Deletion — Jib

Jib lets you delete your account and all associated data directly within the app — no email or web request needed.

## How to delete your account

1. Open Jib and go to **Settings**
2. Scroll to the bottom and tap **Delete account**
3. Confirm by tapping **Yes, delete**

That's it — deletion is immediate.

## What gets deleted

Deleting your account permanently removes, both from your device and from our cloud storage:

- Every house, space, and job you've created
- All photos (house, space, and job photos, including before/after pairs)
- Your shopping lists and inventory
- Your AI-generated home summary and job plans
- Your account's association with your sign-in method (Google, Apple, or email/password), if you signed in

This action cannot be undone.

## What isn't deleted

Deleting your account through Jib removes all of Jib's own data about you. It does not delete or affect your underlying Google Account or Apple ID — Jib uses Google or Apple Sign-In only to authenticate you and sync your data, and doesn't have the ability to delete a Google Account or Apple ID, only the data Jib itself stored. If you signed in with email/password, that account (email + password) is also deleted as part of this.

## No account? No problem

If you use Jib without signing in, all your data already lives only on your device — you can remove it at any time simply by uninstalling the app, or by using the same in-app "Delete account" option, which works the same way whether or not you're signed in.
